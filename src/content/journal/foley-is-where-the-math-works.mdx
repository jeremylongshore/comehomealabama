When people call me about moving to the coast, most of them start by asking about Gulf
Shores or Orange Beach. About half of them end up loving Foley instead. Not because they
settled. Because the math worked, and the life worked with it.

## What Foley actually is

Foley is the working center of south Baldwin County. It sits on Highway 59 about ten miles
north of the beach, which means you can be on the sand in the time it takes to finish a cup
of coffee, but you're not paying gulf-front insurance or resort-district prices to live
there. It has the county's retail spine (Tanger Outlets, the big-box corridor), OWA's
amusement park and events district, a hospital, and one of the busiest sports-tourism
complexes in the region.

It is not a resort town, and that's the point. People who live in Foley mostly live there
year-round. You'll have neighbors in January.

## Why the numbers tend to work

Foley has been one of the most active new-construction markets in Baldwin County for years.
The national builders have multiple communities going at any given time, and that steady
supply does something the beach towns can't: it keeps entry prices within reach. A
brand-new three-bedroom in Foley routinely costs less than a decades-old condo two blocks
off the water, and it comes with a builder warranty instead of a special assessment.

Insurance is the quiet advantage. Every mile you move inland from the gulf matters to an
underwriter. Same storm map, different premium. When buyers compare true monthly cost
instead of purchase price, Foley keeps winning on the spreadsheet.

## What to go in eyes-open about

Honest numbers cut both ways, so here's the other side of it. Highway 59 traffic in summer
is real; locals learn the back ways (the Foley Beach Express is your friend). Some of the
new communities are still building out, which means construction noise for a season and
young trees for a few years. And if what you want is walking out your back door onto the
sand, Foley is not that, and I won't pretend otherwise.

## Who it fits

The buyers I see happiest in Foley: first-time buyers who'd rather own new than rent old,
retirees who want the beach nearby but a normal town around them, people relocating here
for work anywhere in south Baldwin, and condo owners who ran the numbers on their dues and
decided a yard sounded better.
